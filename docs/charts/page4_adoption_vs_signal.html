Adoption vs wholesale signal

Status
33 approved LDCs plotted (33 in correlation). Spearman rho = -0.33 (n = 33).
x-axis: Wholesale locational premium: mean day-ahead LMP minus OZP ($/MWh), load nodes, 2026-09-09 to 2026-10-10. The premium is the LDC's equal-weighted mean over its load nodes of the per-node mean over matched hours of (DA LMP_h - DA OZP_h); nodes need 15 matched days. The premium is mostly marginal losses (about 0.97 correlation with the loss component); congestion is near zero at most nodes in this window. The window is a roughly 32-day shoulder-season sample and not annual. y-axis: net-metered capacity per 1,000 customers (OEB RRR 2.1.2 Table 1 only). Hollow markers are approved LDCs excluded from the correlation (heterogeneous or borrowed nearest node). No causal claim is made. [ANALYST COMMENTARY - TO BE WRITTEN BY AUTHOR]
